Special jointsStanflex

When the duty is anything but standard.

Standard Precision Bellows has great depth of experience in the design and manufacture of special expansion joints. These special joints are available to you:

01

High pressure

For requirements up to 100 kg/cm², reinforced expansion joints are manufactured from relatively thick-gauge bellows of single or multi-ply construction. The roots are ring-reinforced with T-section back-up rings, and the weld ends and centre tubes are heavy-walled and X-ray inspected.

02

Refractory lined

For very high temperatures up to 1300 °C combined with pressures of 1–15 kg/cm². Studs or mesh on a heavy liner carry an air-cured or heat-cured refractory to suit the duty — typically flue-gas regenerators, steel-mill blast mains and cement-plant ducting.

03

PTFE (Teflon) lined

Where highly corrosive applications are encountered, we recommend a PTFE-lined joint: the inside of the bellows, and of the weld ends or vanstone faces, is PTFE coated. Applications include pulp & paper, sewage and chemical plants.

04

Deep corrugation

Certain systems call for corrugations of unusual depth. Bellows with corrugations over 300 mm deep have been manufactured by Standard Precision Bellows.
