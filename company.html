The companyStanflex

Engineered flexibility for critical piping.

Every piping system must make adequate provision for the expansion and contraction that comes with temperature change — along with distortion, misalignment, vibration and movement in many directions. Metallic bellows expansion joints are the most modern and economical way to absorb these movements: compared with pipe loops and slip joints, they accommodate movement in several directions, eliminate costly maintenance and require far less space.

STANFLEX multi-ply bellows are the result of years of experience. Building the bellows wall from several thin plies gives the combined strength of many layers against internal pressure, while retaining the flexibility and movement capacity of each individual thin ply.

A standard range meets almost every expansion problem in practice — made possible by long experience and an in-house computer design facility that continuously optimises every corrugation parameter for each diameter. Beyond the standard range, we design and manufacture bellows for special applications.
