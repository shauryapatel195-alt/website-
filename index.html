The companyStanflex

Engineered flexibility for critical piping.

Every piping system must make adequate provision for the expansion and contraction that comes with temperature change — along with distortion, misalignment, vibration and movement in many directions. Metallic bellows expansion joints are the most modern and economical way to absorb these movements: compared with pipe loops and slip joints, they accommodate movement in several directions, eliminate costly maintenance and require far less space.

STANFLEX multi-ply bellows are the result of years of experience. Building the bellows wall from several thin plies gives the combined strength of many layers against internal pressure, while retaining the flexibility and movement capacity of each individual thin ply.

A standard range meets almost every expansion problem in practice — made possible by long experience and an in-house computer design facility that continuously optimises every corrugation parameter for each diameter. Beyond the standard range, we design and manufacture bellows for special applications.

EngineeringStanflex

Corrugation geometry & multi-ply construction.

The shape of every corrugation is decided by a small set of parameters, continuously optimised for each diameter by our in-house design facility:

  • d inside diameter
  • h corrugation height
  • ri lower corrugation radius
  • ra upper corrugation radius
  • p pitch
  • s total wall thickness
  • n number of plies
  • t unit ply thickness

For a corrugation of fixed shape, increasing the thickness of a single ply reduces the stress due to pressure — but increases the stress due to movement. Multi-ply construction overcomes this limitation and meets special pressure and travel requirements together. STANFLEX manufactures:

Single-ply bellows

One wall of optimised thickness. Available in all sizes, for duties where pressure and movement requirements are moderate.

Multi-ply bellows

Two or more thin plies formed by an exclusive corrugating process. The plies share the pressure load while each stays thin and flexible — suitable for the most critical pressure and movement problems.

ConfigurationsStanflex

Nine ways to absorb movement.

Axial, universal, lateral and angular arrangements — supplied with weld ends, flanged ends or vanstone faces, plain or pressure balanced, to suit the layout of your line. The arrows show the primary movement each arrangement absorbs.

Axial

Absorbs axial extension and compression within a single bellows element.

Pressure balanced axial

Balancing bellows and tie rods cancel the pressure thrust, protecting equipment and light anchors.

Pressure balanced axial, with elbow

Pressure-balanced action arranged at a change of direction in the line.

Universal

Two bellows with a centre spool absorb large lateral movement together with axial travel.

Pressure balanced universal

Universal action with the pressure thrust balanced out of the system.

Pressure balanced universal, with elbow

Combined lateral absorption and thrust balance at an elbow or tee.

Lateral

Tie-rod restrained; absorbs lateral offset while the rods carry the pressure thrust.

Spherical lateral

Spherical tie-rod seats allow lateral movement in any plane.

Angular (hinged)

Hinges restrict movement to angular rotation in one plane; normally used in sets of two or three.

Special jointsStanflex

When the duty is anything but standard.

Standard Precision Bellows has great depth of experience in the design and manufacture of special expansion joints. These special joints are available to you:

01

High pressure

For requirements up to 100 kg/cm², reinforced expansion joints are manufactured from relatively thick-gauge bellows of single or multi-ply construction. The roots are ring-reinforced with T-section back-up rings, and the weld ends and centre tubes are heavy-walled and X-ray inspected.

02

Refractory lined

For very high temperatures up to 1300 °C combined with pressures of 1–15 kg/cm². Studs or mesh on a heavy liner carry an air-cured or heat-cured refractory to suit the duty — typically flue-gas regenerators, steel-mill blast mains and cement-plant ducting.

03

PTFE (Teflon) lined

Where highly corrosive applications are encountered, we recommend a PTFE-lined joint: the inside of the bellows, and of the weld ends or vanstone faces, is PTFE coated. Applications include pulp & paper, sewage and chemical plants.

04

Deep corrugation

Certain systems call for corrugations of unusual depth. Bellows with corrugations over 300 mm deep have been manufactured by Standard Precision Bellows.

InstallationStanflex

Basic installation data.

  1. The pipe must be straight for proper installation, and only one expansion joint shall be used between two anchors.
  2. Pipe anchors must be rigid and capable of withstanding the full pressure-thrust load, including a safety factor of at least 4:1.
  3. Install proper pipe guides so only axial motion is allowed — the first within four pipe diameters of the joint, the second within fourteen diameters of the first.
  4. When field welds are made, ground to the pipe only — never earth the welding set through the expansion joint, and do not allow arcing.
  5. Remove shipping bars after installation.
  6. Pre-compression may be required if the installation temperature is considerably higher than the minimum temperature of the line.
  7. Joints are liquid-penetrant or hydrostatically tested at manufacture — do not hammer test.
