STANFLEX multi-ply expansion joints, standard design. Pick the working-pressure series, then filter by nominal bore or part number.

New to expansion joints? How to read this table
Part No.
Encodes the joint: 100 18 06 = NB 100 mm bore, length code 18, rated for the 6 kg/cm² series.
NB
Nominal bore — the pipe size the joint is made for. Match it to your pipe's NB and you can't go far wrong.
I.D. / O.D.
Inside and outside diameter of the bellows itself.
OAL
Overall length as supplied — quoted separately for weld ends and flanged ends, so you know the gap it needs in the line.
Axial ext. + / comp. −
How far the joint can safely stretch (+) or squeeze (−) from its free length as the pipe moves.
Total travel
Extension and compression added together — the full working movement. Pick a row where this comfortably exceeds the movement you calculated.
Spring rate
The force (kg) needed to move the joint 1 mm. Lower = more flexible = less load on your anchors.
Eff. area
The area the line pressure acts on. Pressure × this area is the thrust your pipe anchors must resist.

Choosing a joint in three steps

  1. Match the bore. Find the rows whose NB equals your pipe size.
  2. Match the pressure. Use the 6 kg/cm² series or switch to the 10 kg/cm² series if your working pressure is higher.
  3. Match the movement. Pick the row whose total travel covers the thermal movement of your line, with margin.

Not sure about your movement or pressure? Send us the service conditions — line size, medium, pressure, temperature — and we will size the joint for you.
