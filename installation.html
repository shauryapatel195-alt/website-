InstallationStanflex

Basic installation data.

  1. The pipe must be straight for proper installation, and only one expansion joint shall be used between two anchors.
  2. Pipe anchors must be rigid and capable of withstanding the full pressure-thrust load, including a safety factor of at least 4:1.
  3. Install proper pipe guides so only axial motion is allowed — the first within four pipe diameters of the joint, the second within fourteen diameters of the first.
  4. When field welds are made, ground to the pipe only — never earth the welding set through the expansion joint, and do not allow arcing.
  5. Remove shipping bars after installation.
  6. Pre-compression may be required if the installation temperature is considerably higher than the minimum temperature of the line.
  7. Joints are liquid-penetrant or hydrostatically tested at manufacture — do not hammer test.
