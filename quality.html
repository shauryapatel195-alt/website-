Quality assuranceStanflex

Tested before it ever sees your line.

At STANFLEX, skilled people and a great deal of equipment are employed to ensure a high level of workmanship. Automatic TIG welding with programmable equipment ensures the same precision throughout the whole range of bellows production — and stabilised type 321 stainless steel is the standard bellows material.

  1. A

    Materials

    Chemical and mechanical analysis of the materials for bellows, pipe ends, flanges and accessories.

  2. B

    Welds

    Radiography and dye-penetrant tests on the welding of the cylinder to bellows, bellows to pipe ends, and hardware.

  3. C

    Behaviour

    Spring-rate and life-cycle testing of the bellows.

  4. D

    Complete unit

    Internal pressure testing of the finished joint — hydraulic, pneumatic and helium leak tests.

Helium leak detection

Every bellows undergoes a high-vacuum test in which, thanks to the molecular structure of helium gas, the sensor picks up any minute fault in the bellows unit.

No limits on size or shape

Sizes and shapes are no limitation — bellows can be supplied from 15 mm up to 5 metres in diameter.

Certified acceptance

Acceptance tests are carried out by us or by outside inspection organisations, and test certificates are issued whenever acceptance-test requirements are specified in the order.

Shape, size, pressure and temperature are no bar to us.
